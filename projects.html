Vikas Rubbers / Floormate — ERP Software

Manufacturing / Rubber Products & Flooring Manufacturing ERP / Business Management ERP

Rubber Molding & Flooring Production, Shop-Floor Operations, Global Export Orders & Dispatch Logistics

📋 Project Overview & Domain Scope

A comprehensive manufacturing and business management ERP engineered for Vikas Rubbers / Floormate to manage end-to-end operations across rubber product molding, flooring material production, raw material purchasing, warehouse inventory, quality control, customer export accounts, and shipment dispatch.

As a QA Engineer, my role focused on validating complex multi-stage production workflows, inventory valuation, bill of materials (BOM), supplier orders, quality inspection tolerances, and international export shipping documentation.

🏢 Business Domain Pillars

🔘 Rubber Products Manufacturing

Compound mixing ratios, vulcanization molding cycles, industrial rubber sheets, custom profile extrusions, and rubber scrap recycling workflows.

🧱 Flooring Products

Commercial gym tiles, ballistic tiles, interlocking playground pavers, acoustic underlays, and specialized anti-static flooring surfaces.

⚙️ Manufacturing & Plant Operations

Daily production scheduling, machine load balancing, operator shift tracking, mold maintenance logs, and downtime monitoring.

🚢 International Sales / Export Business

Global export clients, multi-currency invoicing, container volume calculations, customs packing lists, and export freight tracking.

🔄 End-to-End Manufacturing & Supply Chain Workflow Tested (12 Stages)

Login → Dashboard → Sales → Purchase → Inventory → Production → Quality → Accounts → Customer → Supplier → Reports → Dispatch

⚙️ Key Modules & Functional Scope

Sales & Customer Management
  • Sales Inquiries & Quotations: Validated export cost sheets, pricing tiers, and formal quote generation.
  • Customer Orders: Verified proforma invoice conversion, order confirmation, and delivery schedules.
  • Customer Master: Validated KYC records, GSTIN / VAT compliance, credit limits, and contact directories.
Purchase & Supplier Management
  • Purchase Orders (PO): Tested raw material purchase requisitions, chemical compound orders, and approval workflows.
  • Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Verified warehouse inward material logging, weight validation, and quality sampling.
  • Supplier Master: Maintained vendor catalogs, lead times, payment terms, and vendor performance logs.
Inventory & Production Management
  • Inventory Control: Validated raw materials, WIP, and finished tiles stock levels across warehouse bins.
  • Bill of Materials (BOM): Verified precise chemical and rubber scrap mix formulas per square meter.
  • Production Orders: Validated press-molding job cards, shift outputs, machine assignments, and downtime logs.
Quality Control, Accounts & Dispatch
  • Quality Inspection (QC): Validated density tests, tensile strength checks, thickness tolerances, and rejection logs.
  • Accounts & Invoicing: Tested multi-currency billing, tax ledgers, supplier payments, and credit notes.
  • Logistics & Dispatch: Verified packing slips, container stuffing manifests, export gate passes, and carrier tracking.

🔄 QA TESTING METHODOLOGY & COVERAGE

Requirement Analysis → BOM & Formula Testing → Shop-Floor Workflow → Quality Inspection Logic → Inventory Synchronization → Export Invoicing → Regression Testing → Defect Management → Release Validation
